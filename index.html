Dr Anne Baranger

Forest ecologist · Postdoctoral Research Associate, Lines Research Group

My research focuses on how European forests respond to climate change. I currently combine terrestrial LiDAR with forest inventory data from permanent plots across Europe to study how tree canopy shape affects light in the understory and the dynamics of young trees.

I did my PhD at the University of Grenoble Alpes, where I studied the vulnerability of European forests to climate change: resilience after disturbances, sensitivity to frost and drought, and reproduction.

Open to collaboration. If you are interested in working together, please get in touch at amob2 (at) cam.ac.uk.
